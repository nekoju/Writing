A Witness


A witness in the sense, perhaps, of seeing what you've done
A lover in the sense of their profession
Another sense, the sense of really seeing what you are
Obscured by tepid happy self reflection

Your champion in the sense, perhaps, of bailing out their boat
Which hap's to keep your sorry ass dry too
A runner-up, I'm so afraid to have to break it down
In caring where the dinghy's taking you

And advocate, you might expect, in finding you the care
For keeping all your options lucr'd up
A prosecutor for the times you wake up unawares
Of what the fine print's telling you is love

A protector and a listener
Your wanderer abreast
Refuge and your safety from the wind
Your embracer, your provider
And finally, yes your witness
If and only if it's benefitting them
