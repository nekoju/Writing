Lamentation for the gifted


Would I had a time machine
To go into the past
And dig up the unpleasantries
They thought would make me last
The wasting of potential
And the living in the now
Immune to broader questions
Of "Wonderful, but how?"
I'd show them all the colors
'hind the sockets of my eyes
And I'd show them all the truths they said
Miasma'd with the lies
I'd stand above them hunching
In a soul without a form
And ask them if their great successes
Could deign to keep them warm
The ones who could behave themselves
The ones who soldiered on
The ones who stared, belabored
At the scourge of imposition, fawned
The ones who did the figures
The ones who saw the trees
The ones who came up looking
Over better skies than me
