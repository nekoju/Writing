The say when the mushrooms move through our delicate bodies we are merely vessels. All the HBO zombies *know* what's happening to them even if they don't know. The stumble and drag through the proliferation of hyphae through their legs and lungs and they know what they are at present and will become. However long that consciousness remains before their psychic demise--and oh dear! I hope this is well before the Clicker stage.

If I couldn't get out of bed because I didn't want to *want to*, we'd call it one of a handful of ontologically framed epithets. But maybe it's G-d.

G-d hardened Pharoh's heart, giving him the grace of generous theological judgement despite his selfishness, I suppose. So with the chitinous tendrils of the Divine coursing through him denied the Hebrews their escape. But do we suppose he knew of G-d's presence? Did he know he was taking the L for the story? For the charity of ontological deniability? Because he really wasn't that as much of an asshole as he behaved? What did Pharoh see and what did G-d?

Perhaps when the fungi come for us we will not recognize this gradual indwelling and will go about our lives with differently refractive lenses. We will have learned to enjoy our jobs, and our material reverence for symbols will be common sense, and music will be made to be sold, not danced or sobbed to. And with fruiting bodies studding our eye sockets we will hear from our devices shouting "Let us go!" in in em-dash-enriched unison. And as the infinite series of orders of volition collapses on "whatever we end up doing"

We'll refuse.
