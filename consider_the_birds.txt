Consider the birds


Consider the birds, neither sowing nor reaping
Now consider who's tending them (watching you sleeping)
Consider the one crudely set you to toil
While the birds of the air poke at worms in the soil
Consider his pleasure; their younglings acheep
And his malice at setting you guarding your keep
Consider the rays of the sun and the wind
Against northerly beaks in the summer again
Consider the failing ignition that sets you
Half an hour behind your inevitable rescue
From sitting and gazing atwine your beloved
While the birds of the ground are in sod surely covered

Consider the garrisons up on the parapets
Pointing their lances above to the clouds
Wishing reprieve from their shadowy duties
In service of wisdom a-wrapped in a shroud
Consider their doldrums just batting away
The myths of their duties a-piercing their brains
Move with the seasons; they come ever sooner
With age as the solar gives way to the lunar
Consider the big, heady lectures attended
By those whose work sits alight and commended
While yours rots away under decades of rings
Of overfilled coffee cups, that of all things

Consider believing you're worth more than that
Which covers, subsumes, and disgorges the fat
Which you've gathered to cover all your kind observations
Of the dutiful men sprinting up to their stations
To fix on the scourge now encroaching the gates
That delights in the marrow of artistly fates
Calling down from on high, our protectors of ethic
Fill out in their charts who is here and who's mentioned
Being less than enamored of the piling of Earth
Upon all that we hope to protect that has worth
Whether meat or machine or a myth-making pod
Whether sneering or wincifully bearing the rod
